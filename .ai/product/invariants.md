## Non-Negotiables

- Local-only audio/transcript/summary processing.
- No cloud transcription, remote summary APIs, telemetry of meeting content, or meeting-content logging.
- macOS Apple Silicon only unless product direction explicitly changes.
- Human Product Owner owns product direction and acceptance.
